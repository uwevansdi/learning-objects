The can that was kicked: Portland Public Schools' budget gaps, 2022–2026

Portland Public Schools (PPS), Oregon's largest district, has faced a budget shortfall five years running. This case moves through three moments when district leaders made choices. At each one, decide what you would do and why, then see what actually happened. The epilogue covers what comes next.

    About this case and its sources
    • A real case. Facts are drawn from news reporting and district materials through June 2026 and linked at each stage. Where sources differ, for example $13.1M versus $13.2M for the staffing amendment, the case uses a round figure. Check for later developments before teaching.
    • Neutral framing. Quotes from district leaders, board members, and others are taken from published reporting. The case is meant to support debate, not to assign blame.
    • Privacy. Your choices and notes stay in this browser tab. Nothing is saved or sent, and reloading clears them.
    • Accessibility. Every control works by keyboard. The chart's values are also listed in the text.
