Public Service Delivery Exercise: plan transit for your community

Your group is a planning team for one Seattle community. With limited resources, design a new bus line: decide what your community needs most, choose stations along a route the bus can actually run, and see who can walk to it and which services they can reach. Partway through, an implementation challenge will force you to revise. At the end, copy your plan into your group's Word document.

Draft for discussion with Prof. Zhao. Streets and service locations come from OpenStreetMap; population, race, and income come from census tracts. Community notes, roles, rules, and shocks are placeholders to align with the instructor's worksheet and community profiles.

      About this draft and its data
      • Streets and services: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License. Walking uses streets, stairways, and trails; the bus uses streets only and prefers arterials. Service locations include food banks and pantries, health clinics, and libraries and community centers mapped in OpenStreetMap; coverage is incomplete and should be checked against local directories.
      • Population: City of Seattle, Racial and Social Equity Composite Index (2026 release, ACS 5-year estimates), by census tract: total population, people of color, and people below 200% of the poverty level ("lower income"). Residents are spread evenly along each tract's residential streets.
      • Walking and travel: a 10-minute walk is 800 meters along the street network. Trip times assume an average bus speed of 18 km/h including stops. Hills are not yet accounted for.
      • Candidate stations are major arterial intersections at least 700 meters apart, chosen automatically. Prof. Zhao may replace them with his own list.
      • Privacy. This page collects no data. Group names and notes stay in this browser tab and go only into the summary you copy or download.
      • Accessibility. Every step works by keyboard. Stations can be added, removed, and reordered from the lists; the scorecard states every result in text.
