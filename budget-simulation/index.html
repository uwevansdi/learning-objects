Closing the gap: a county budget simulation

You are the new budget director for Cedar County, a fictional Washington county with an $80 million general fund. Make four decisions over three budget years. Each choice changes the county's reserves, services, public trust, and tax bills, and early choices limit later ones. There is no single right answer.

About this simulation
  • The real constraint: Washington counties can raise their regular property tax levy by at most 1% a year without voter approval. A levy lid lift asks voters to allow more and passes with a simple majority. When costs grow faster than 1%, a structural gap opens.
  • Fictional county, simplified rules. Cedar County's numbers, and how trust turns into votes, are teaching assumptions, not predictions. Real budgets involve labor contracts, state mandates, and many more choices.
  • Privacy. This page collects no data. It sets no cookies, uses no storage, and makes no network requests after it loads. Reloading starts over.
  • Accessibility. Every choice is a keyboard-operable button. After each decision, focus moves to the outcome, and the dashboard changes are listed in text.
