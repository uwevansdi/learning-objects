Smoke season: who should act, and when?

A ready-to-run, 60-minute live session on wildfire smoke policy in Washington, with polls, data slides built on EPA air quality data, a timed small-group case, and a printable handout. Every question and prompt can be edited, and your version can be shared as a link.

Your edits live in this page's link, not on any server. Bookmark the link, or send it to a colleague so they can start from your version. Nothing about students is collected.

About this kit
  • Data: U.S. EPA, AirData annual AQI by county, 2014–2025, retrieved October 2026. "Smoke days" here are days rated Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups or worse (AQI above 100) from any pollutant; in Washington's high-AQI summer days, the main driver is wildfire smoke (fine particles, PM2.5).
  • Coverage: a county appears only if it had monitoring data that year, and some counties have partial-year coverage.
  • Workplace rule: Washington L&I's permanent wildfire smoke rules took effect January 15, 2024 (L&I rulemaking page).
  • Polls without student devices. Enter counts from a show of hands, or run the question in Poll Everywhere for phone responses and enter the totals here. Counts stay in this browser tab and are cleared on reload; the kit itself never collects student responses.
  • Accessibility. All modes work by keyboard. Charts list their values in the handout tables.
